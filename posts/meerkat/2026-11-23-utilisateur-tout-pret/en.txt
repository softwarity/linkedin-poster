Your services don't need to know how the user signed in. They need to know who the user is, and to be able to trust it.

Meerkat is an app-gateway: a single door, with its own access control, in front of your company's internal applications.

A familiar story: an internal application built from microservices. Every service ends up rebuilding its own slice of identity: a session, a role check, sometimes a users table. The second factor is handled somewhere else, or not at all. And no two services ever agree on what a user is.

With Meerkat, all of this happens once, at the door, on the gateway's sign-in pages, in your colours:
→ local accounts, OpenID Connect or GitHub
→ a TOTP second factor, with backup codes and trusted browsers
→ passkeys as a first factor, with no username or password
→ with several tenants, the person picks theirs when signing in

Then every service receives a signed JWT in Authorization: Bearer. Any Authorization header the client sent is removed first, so nobody can forge one. The token carries the account (sub), plus whatever the route chose to send: username, e-mail, roles, tenant. It lives two minutes by default.

To verify it, the service needs no secret, no accounts table, and no call back to the gateway. Just one address, on the internal plane:

http://meerkat:7070/.well-known/openid-configuration

Common libraries use it as is: MetadataAddress in .NET, openid-client in Node, issuer-uri in Spring with the issuer that document announces. Key rotation reaches them on its own.

The no-code option: the route can send the same identity as headers, for an application that already reads a user header.

The result: your services no longer ship a login page, a role model or a users table. They read who is calling, their roles and their tenant as a fact, not as a parameter chosen by the caller.

All of this is in the Community edition, free, production included, except multiple tenants and SAML, LDAP or Active Directory, which are Enterprise. To try it, the evaluation edition:

docker run -p 8080:8080 -p 9090:9090 -e MEERKAT_ADMIN_PASSWORD=choose-one softwarity/meerkat

https://meerkat.softwarity.dev

In your services, who checks identity today? Each service in its own way, a shared library, or the gateway?
