Animation: an API gateway in front of an application's services. Around it the stack piles up: an IAM, an auth proxy, a developer tunnel, an OpenAPI UI per service, configuration files, and every brick shipping its own telemetry, so the traffic tangles. The gateway absorbs it all and turns into Meerkat, and the title API-GATEWAY becomes APP-GATEWAY. Traffic then flows through Meerkat to each service, and metrics, traces and logs leave from a single point to the OpenTelemetry collector.
